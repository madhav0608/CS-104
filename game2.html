Rules for 2 player game

  • Player 2 has to set the fielders first.
  • Player 1 bats first.
    There will be no different scores on each cell in this mode.
    On each square selected which is not a fielder you score 1 run.
  • After player 1 has finished batting , player 1 needs to set the fielding.
  • Player 2 needs to bat now.
    The winner is decided on who scores more.
